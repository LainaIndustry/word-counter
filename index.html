What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that calculates the number of words in a text. It can also measure characters, sentences and paragraphs. Writers, students, teachers, bloggers and professionals use word counters to meet writing requirements and review documents.

How to Use This Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the editor.
  2. Review the word, character, sentence and paragraph counts.
  3. Check keyword frequency and estimated reading time.
  4. Set a writing goal if you want to track your progress.
  5. Copy or download your text when you finish.

Why Word Count Matters

Word limits are important for assignments, essays, articles, reports, applications and social media posts. Checking word count as you write helps you stay within the required length and plan your content more effectively.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count words online?

Paste or type your text into the editor. The tool automatically calculates the word count.

What is the difference between words and characters?

Words are units of text separated by whitespace. Characters include letters, numbers, punctuation and spaces.

Can I count characters without spaces?

Yes. The No Spaces statistic excludes whitespace characters from the character count.

Does this tool save my text?

This version processes text in your browser and does not send the editor content to a server. Your text is not saved automatically.
