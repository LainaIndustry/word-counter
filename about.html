About WordCounter

WordCounter is an online text analysis tool designed to help users understand the length and basic structure of their writing.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make everyday writing tasks easier for students, teachers, bloggers, content creators and professionals.

Our Tools

The website provides word counting, character counting, sentence counting, paragraph counting, keyword frequency analysis and estimated reading time.

Privacy and Simplicity

The current version processes text in your browser and does not upload the editor contents to a server. Text is not saved automatically.
